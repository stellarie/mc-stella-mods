# Stella Nether Raid

An instanced nether raid. The design is in `docs/plans/2026-09-28/NETHER-RAID.md`.

## Slice 0: scaffold

- The mod requires the rest of the modpack: mobs, equips and enchants, spells, villages, the tower and food.
  Gradle builds each from its sibling directory as an included build, so build from this directory with
  the siblings checked out beside it.

## Slice 1: the raid dimension and the portal choice

- A second nether, `mod-stella-nether:raid`, generated by the nether's own noise and biomes.
- A player stepping into an overworld nether portal is held there and asked in chat: **[Normal nether]**
  or **[Raid nether]**. Clicking runs `/stellanether portal normal|raid`. The next crossing takes them
  there. A choice covers one crossing and lapses after 600 ticks (30 seconds). The player is asked again
  at most every 100 ticks while they stay in the portal.
- In survival, the prompt comes after vanilla's 4-second portal wait, and the crossing after another
  4 seconds.
- Mobs and other entities are never asked. They cross to the normal nether as in vanilla. A player riding
  a mount decides for the mount.
- The raid arrival is an obsidian hall at (0, 64, 1) with a lit portal out at its north end. Lava in the
  hall's shell is turned to blackstone.
- Inside the raid nether, every nether portal leads back beside the portal the player came in by. With no
  recorded entrance, it leads to their respawn point. Scrolls of Return work unchanged.

## Slice 2: host and mode

- The raid nether is one shared instance. Its state (run, open, host, mode, time run) is saved on the
  Overworld.
- While no raid is open, the portal prompt offers **[Host raid: normal]** and **[Host raid: hard]**; the
  first player through opens the raid, hosts it, and sets its mode. While a raid is open, it offers
  **[Join the raid (mode)]** instead, and a later player joins without changing host or mode.
- Every player inside holds one boon, drawn at random from speed, haste, strength, jump boost,
  regeneration, resistance, fire resistance, night vision, absorption, health boost, slow falling and
  luck. It lasts while they stay inside, and leaving takes it away. A potion of the same kind that is
  not endless is left alone.
- In hard mode, every hostile mob that loads in the raid holds two different boons, drawn from speed,
  strength, resistance, regeneration, absorption, health boost and fire resistance. Only effects the mob
  can hold are drawn, so undead are never handed regeneration. A mob is boosted once per run.
- Each run has its own site; see slice 5.

## Slice 3: the instance lifecycle

- The open raid counts its ticks, saved with its state.
- Once no player has stood in the raid for 100 ticks (5 seconds), it resets. Dying, a Scroll of Return, a
  portal and logging out all count as leaving. Loaded dropped items in the raid are discarded, and the
  next run opens 2048 blocks away, so everything left on the old run's land is lost for good.
- A player who logs back in inside a run that has since closed is sent back the way they came. A player
  who logs back in inside the live run stays.
- The old runs' land stays on disk. The raid dimension's region files grow by one explored area per run,
  and pruning them is a follow-up.

## Slice 4: the enrage

- At 30 minutes (36,000 ticks) of open raid, everyone inside is warned, and every hostile mob in the raid
  gains endless Speed II and Strength II and 64 more blocks of follow range. Zombified piglins count.
  A mob that loads later in the same run is enraged as it loads.
- Once a second, an enraged mob with no player to hunt takes the nearest survival or adventure player
  within 64 blocks. A neutral mob stays angry at them.
- The enrage lasts until the raid resets.

## Slice 5: a fortress and a bastion in reach

- Each run's origin lies 8,192 blocks from the last (800 runs per row, inside the nether's world border).
- The run opens midway between a fortress and a bastion of the raid nether's own generation that lie no
  more than 480 blocks apart, so both structure starts are within 256 blocks of the arrival. Nothing is
  forced into the land. The search reads the actual structure start in each placement cell within three
  cells of the origin (vanilla's locate is not used: it answers with the first ring holding a match, not
  the nearest), and takes the pair nearest the origin.
- Measured: 60 of 60 runs over five suite runs had both in reach (by a scan of every chunk within 256
  blocks), with no fallback. The same check at the bare origin, before this slice, gave 2 of 6.
- The search runs on the server thread the first time a run is needed, costing up to about 2 seconds
  (worst measured 2,044 ms) once per run.
- A server with structures switched off has no fortress or bastion to find; the run then opens at its
  origin and logs a warning.

## Slice 6a: the raid's threat level

- The mobs mod gives a dimension it does not know no threat floor, so raid mobs rolled threat 0 to 4,
  weaker than the normal nether's 20. Every mob that loads in the raid now has its threat lifted to start
  from the nether's floor (`ThreatState.NETHER_MINIMUM`), keeping its own roll above that, and is scaled
  again by the mobs mod's `SpawnScaling`. Measured: eight raid zombified piglins at levels 20 to 28,
  40 to 48 health.
- Fixed: the reset discarded items while walking the level's entity map, which skipped 23 of 64 items
  and once crashed the server tick (`ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` in `RaidInstance.tick`). Both
  loops over the raid's entities now walk a snapshot.

## Manual acceptance

- Walk into an overworld nether portal in survival. The prompt appears, and clicking **[Raid nether]**
  takes you to the hall. The headless test player is never ticked, so the tests call the portal's
  destination directly and cannot walk through it.
- The prompt options are coloured, and hovering shows what each one means. The options change once a
  raid is open.
- A boon shows in the inventory's effect list as endless while inside, and is gone after leaving.
- From a new run's arrival, a fortress and a bastion are both in sight within a short walk.
- After the enrage, a crowd of zombified piglins actually chases a player back to the hall's portal.
  The test proves the targeting, not the pathing through nether terrain.
